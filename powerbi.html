Business Requirement

To analyze Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution using KPIs and visualizations in Power BI.

KPI Requirements

  1. Total Sales: Total revenue generated from items sold.
  2. Average Sales: Average sales value.
  3. Number of Items: Total number of items sold.
  4. Average Rating: Average customer rating.

Chart Requirements

  1. Total Sales by Fat Content — Donut Chart

    Compare sales and other KPIs by fat content.

  2. Total Sales by Item Type — Bar Chart

    Compare sales across different item types.

  3. Fat Content by Outlet — Stacked Column Chart

    Compare outlet sales by fat content.

  4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment — Line Chart

    Analyze sales based on outlet establishment year.

  5. Sales by Outlet Size — Donut Chart

    Compare sales across outlet sizes.

  6. Sales by Outlet Location — Funnel Map

    Analyze sales across different locations.

  7. All Metrics by Outlet Type — Matrix

    Compare sales, average sales, items, and ratings.
